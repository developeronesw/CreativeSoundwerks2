# Creative Soundwerks 2 — development contract

- Work directly on **main**, without creating a feature branch or pull request, as requested by the owner.
- Run TypeScript checks, builds, migrations, and integration/security tests after changes.
- Do not call a feature complete unless its integration test has passed on the intended commit.
- Creator uploads are owned by the authenticated account. Only admins may inspect the global media library.
- Only audio, video and safe raster cover/avatar images may be uploaded; never accept scripts, PHP, JSON, text, SVG or arbitrary documents.
- Any VPS changes require clear deployment notes and a rollback/backup strategy.
- The visual contract is the Creative Soundwerks ivory and champagne-gold design.
